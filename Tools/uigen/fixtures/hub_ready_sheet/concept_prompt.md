UI concept art for a small "party ready" screen of a modern third-person 3D action raid game with a realistic art style. It is opened in a quiet hub area, so the middle of the screen stays open and the game world shows through. A 16:9 landscape layout of a 1920x1080 screen, showing the UI alone.

Visual language (most important — same family as the attached style picture): modern flat graphic design. Matte ink-black (#0B0C0E) translucent bands, even and about 75 % opaque in the middle, with crisp straight top and bottom edges and a smooth fade to fully transparent only over the left and right ends; slim flat blades of pale worn steel (bone white #E9E4D6 along the sharp edge, gently darkening to a cool gray-bone toward the inner spine, a few extremely fine scratch lines) that end in a sharp, gently curved, needle-like blade tip; slender crescents. One accent only: pale ivory (#F3E9CC). Lots of empty space. Absolutely no bevels, no embossing, no metal frames, no 3D relief, no thick outlines, no filigree, no gems, no stars, no runes, no astronomical motifs, no purple, no blue, no gold, no red, no orange, no glow. Nothing that looks like a 2D side-scroller or a mobile game.

Draw these UI elements, nothing else (no characters, no scene, no icons):
