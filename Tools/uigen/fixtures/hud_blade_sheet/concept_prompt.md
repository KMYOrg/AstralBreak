UI concept art for the full combat HUD layout of a modern third-person 3D action raid game with a realistic art style — the hero wears a long black leather coat and fights with a black steel scythe against heavy armored guardians in an old sealed ruin. A 16:9 landscape layout of a 1920x1080 screen, showing the HUD alone.

Visual language (most important): modern flat graphic HUD design, like the interface of a contemporary cinematic 3D action game. Matte ink-black (#0B0C0E) translucent bands that fade out smoothly to fully transparent at their ends, thin 1–2 px bone-white (#E9E4D6) hairlines, and slender crescent shapes: lines end in a sharp, gently curved blade tip, like the edge of a scythe. One accent only: pale ivory (#F3E9CC). Lots of empty space. Absolutely no bevels, no embossing, no metal frames, no 3D relief, no thick outlines, no filigree, no gems, no stars, no runes, no astronomical motifs, no purple, no blue, no gold, no red, no orange, no strong glow. Nothing that looks like a 2D side-scroller or a mobile game.

Draw these HUD elements, nothing else (no characters, no faces, no enemies, no weapons, no scene). The middle of the screen stays open:
1. Top left — player status, 48 px from the left and 44 px from the top: a thin horizontal bar about 400 x 10 px filled with bone white, its right end cut into a blade tip; under it a thinner bar about 300 x 5 px filled halfway with pale ivory on a dark track. To their left a simple thin open crescent line as an empty portrait slot, about 72 px. Below, three much smaller identical teammate rows (bar about 240 x 6 px with a 3 px bar under it), each about 60 px apart.
2. Top center, 5 % down — a boss bar about 700 x 10 px, centered: a straight thin bar filled with bone white on a dark translucent track, both ends tapering into blade tips; under it, centered, a very thin bar about 300 x 4 px.
3. Top right, 48 px from the edges — an objective line: an ink-black band about 340 x 40 px that fades out to the left, with one thin bone hairline under it.
4. Upper center, 19 % down — an announcement banner about 620 x 84 px, the largest band: ink-black translucent band, strongest in the middle and fading out to both sides, with one long thin bone hairline along its top and one along its bottom; each hairline curves outward and ends in a sharp blade tip. Its surface is empty.
5. Center, 52 % down — a thin ring about 440 px in diameter: two slender crescent blades curving around the same circle, each thick (about 8 px) in its middle and tapering to a sharp point, the point of one meeting the tail of the other, bone white. The inside of the ring is completely empty and transparent. Inside the ring, in its lower third and clearly separated from the ring, a small ink-black translucent band about 240 x 112 px that fades out to its left and right, with no frame, its surface empty, with room for a small title, a large number and a smaller count.
6. Lower center, 82 % down — a small emblem about 56 px: one simple upright scythe-blade crescent in pale ivory, with a long hair-thin ivory line about 380 px wide passing horizontally behind it and fading out at both ends.
7. Bottom center, 92 % down — a slim horizontal gauge about 520 x 22 px: a dark ink track with a thin bone hairline outline, both ends tapering into blade tips; filled about 70 % from the left with solid pale ivory, ending in a straight vertical edge; the remaining 30 % is clearly empty and dark.
8. Bottom left, 48 px from the edges — two simple thin circles about 56 px side by side, hairline only, as empty item slots.
9. Bottom right, 48 px from the edges — four empty skill slots: thin hairline squares rotated 45 degrees, about 60 px, set along one gentle crescent curve; one short ink-black fading band beside each.

Keep at least 45 px of completely empty space between neighboring elements in the center column (boss bar, banner, ring, emblem, gauge): nothing touches or overlaps. Elements are crisp, sharp and evenly lit, no drop shadows larger than 3 px.

High legibility over bright and dark scenes. No watermark, no logo, no signature.
