UI concept art for the combat HUD of a dark fantasy four-player co-op action RPG — a 16:9 landscape layout of a 1920x1080 screen, showing the HUD alone.

Only these four HUD elements, nothing else (no minimap, no health bars, no ability icons, no chat, no crosshair, no quest text):
1. Bottom center, about 64 px above the bottom edge: a horizontal ultimate gauge about 460 x 44 px — an ornate thin gold frame with small filigree corners around a dark inner track, filled about 70 % from the left with warm gold (#E8B54A), with a soft golden glow.
2. Just above the gauge, centered: leave a little empty transparent space — the game shows a short warning there later, nothing is drawn there now.
3. Top right, about 48 px from the right edge and 140 px from the top: a chain panel about 260 x 132 px — a dark translucent purple-black panel with a thin gold border and small corner ornaments. Its inside is left empty, with room from top to bottom for a small title, a large number and a smaller count.
4. Top center, about 96 px from the top: a burst banner about 420 x 72 px — a dark brown translucent rounded banner with a thin gold outline. Its surface is left empty, with room for a label on the left and a large number on the right.

Style: dark fantasy, gold trim, thin elegant frames, subtle glow, crisp thin lines and ornaments, high legibility. No watermark, no logo, no signature.
